How are these numbers calculated?

Points left: event total minus your current progress.

Daily goal: remaining points divided by estimated playing days before the event ends (minimum 1 day on the final day).

Daily battles: points still needed after daily missions, divided by your average points per battle and rounded up.

With / without missions: total remaining battles with or without subtracting expected daily mission points.

Playtime: estimated battles multiplied by 7 minutes. Queue times, garage time and breaks are not included.

Note: The data is for reference. Enter your own values for accurate calculations.
